GOVERNMENT OF INDIA - MINISTRY OF CONSUMER AFFAIRS, FOOD & PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION
DEPARTMENT OF FOOD & PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION & COMMISSION FOR AGRICULTURAL COSTS AND PRICES (CACP)
MINIMUM SUPPORT PRICE (MSP) AND MANDI PROCUREMENT POLICY GUIDELINES

1. OBJECTIVES OF MINIMUM SUPPORT PRICE (MSP)
(a) To ensure remunerative prices to growers for their produce with a view to encouraging higher investment and production.
(b) To protect farmers from acute price volatility, distress sales during harvest gluts, and predatory trade practices.
(c) To evolve a balanced and integrated price structure in the perspective of the overall agricultural economy.
(d) Government policy fixes MSP at a level of at least 1.5 times the all-India weighted average cost of production (Cost A2 + FL).

2. COMMODITIES COVERED UNDER MSP
The Government fixes MSP for 23 mandated agricultural crops:
(a) 7 Cereals: Paddy, Wheat, Barley, Jowar, Bajra, Maize, Ragi.
(b) 5 Pulses: Gram (Chana), Arhar/Tur, Moong, Urad, Lentil (Masur).
(c) 8 Oilseeds: Groundnut, Rapeseed/Mustard, Soyabean, Sesamum, Sunflower seed, Safflower, Nigerseed, Toria.
(d) Commercial Crops: Copra, Sugarcane (Fair and Remunerative Price - FRP), Raw Cotton, Raw Jute.

3. PROCUREMENT AGENCIES AND MANDI OPERATIONS
(a) Procurement is undertaken by central designated agencies such as Food Corporation of India (FCI), NAFED, Cotton Corporation of India (CCI), along with State Civil Supplies Corporations and registered APMC Mandis.
(b) Procurement centers are set up in major production belts and rural APMC yards.
(c) Every registered farmer is entitled to bring FAQ-grade agricultural produce to procurement centers without quantity caps under Decentralized Procurement (DCP).

4. FAIR AVERAGE QUALITY (FAQ) SPECIFICATIONS
(a) Moisture Content Limit:
    - Paddy (Common and Grade A): Maximum permissible moisture content is 17.0%.
    - Wheat: Maximum permissible moisture content is 12.0%.
    - Maize: Maximum moisture content is 14.0%.
    - Soyabean: Maximum moisture content is 12.0%.
(b) Produce exceeding the designated moisture threshold is subject to pro-rata value cuts or must be sun-dried prior to weighment.
(c) Foreign Matter / Inorganics: Permissible limit of up to 1.0% to 2.0% maximum.
(d) Damaged, weeviled, or discolored grains must not exceed 4.0% to 5.0%.

5. MANDATORY PAYMENT TIMELINES AND DIRECT BENEFIT TRANSFER (DBT)
(a) Statutory Payment Deadline: 100% payment for produce procured at MSP must be credited to the farmer's account within 48 to 72 hours of the issuance of the J-Form (Procurement / Mandi Slip).
(b) Payment Mode: Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) directly into the farmer's Aadhaar-seeded commercial bank or cooperative account via Public Financial Management System (PFMS).
(c) Prohibition of Cash or Intermediary Payments: Mandi agents (Arhtiyas) and private traders are legally barred from deducting unauthorized commissions, brokerage, or transportation cuts from the statutory MSP amount.
(d) The J-Form must explicitly record the net weight in quintals, moisture percentage, gross payable amount computed as (Net Weight × MSP Rate), and transaction timestamp.

6. GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL AND APMC DESK
(a) Non-payment, payment delay exceeding 72 hours, or illegal deductions below the declared MSP rate constitutes a statutory violation of State APMC Acts and National Food Security norms.
(b) Farmers may submit formal grievances to the District Procurement Officer, District Collector, or the Mandi Secretary.
(c) Required documentation for dispute resolution:
    - Original J-Form / Weighment Slip issued by the APMC Mandi.
    - Farmer Registration Certificate / Khasra-Girdawari record.
    - Updated bank passbook showing credit shortfall or non-receipt.
    - Copy of Aadhaar Card.
