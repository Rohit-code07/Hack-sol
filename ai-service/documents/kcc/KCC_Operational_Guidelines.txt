GOVERNMENT OF INDIA - MINISTRY OF AGRICULTURE & FARMERS WELFARE
RESERVE BANK OF INDIA & NABARD
KISAN CREDIT CARD (KCC) SCHEME - REVISED OPERATIONAL GUIDELINES

1. OBJECTIVES OF THE KCC SCHEME
The Kisan Credit Card (KCC) scheme aims to provide adequate and timely credit support from the banking system under a single window with flexible and simplified procedures to the farmers for their cultivation and other agricultural needs, including:
(a) To meet the short-term credit requirements for cultivation of crops.
(b) Post-harvest expenses.
(c) Produce marketing loan.
(d) Consumption requirements of farmer household.
(e) Working capital for maintenance of farm assets and activities allied to agriculture.
(f) Investment credit requirement for agriculture and allied activities.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
(a) All Farmers - Individuals/Joint borrowers who are owner cultivators.
(b) Tenant farmers, oral lessees & share croppers.
(c) Self Help Groups (SHGs) or Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) of farmers including tenant farmers, share croppers etc.
(d) Farmers involved in animal husbandry, dairy, poultry, and fisheries are also eligible for working capital credit limits under KCC.

3. SCALE OF FINANCE AND CREDIT LIMIT DETERMINATION
The credit limit under KCC is determined on the basis of:
(a) Scale of Finance (SOF) for the crop approved by the District Level Technical Committee (DLTC) multiplied by the extent of cultivated area.
(b) Plus 10% of the limit towards post-harvest, household, and consumption requirements.
(c) Plus 20% of the limit towards farm asset repairs, maintenance, and crop insurance premium.
(d) Maximum Permissible Limit for 1st Year = (SOF × Area) + 10% + 20%.
(e) For successive years (2nd to 5th year), an automatic 10% escalation in the credit limit is sanctioned per year to account for cost increases and cropping changes.
(f) The KCC credit facility is sanctioned for a period of 5 years with annual review.

4. INTEREST SUBVENTION SCHEME (ISS) AND INCENTIVES
(a) Standard Benchmark Lending Rate: Banks disburse short-term agricultural crop loans up to Rs. 3.00 lakh at a concessional interest rate of 7.00% per annum.
(b) Central Government Interest Subvention: Government of India provides an interest subvention of 1.50% to 2.00% to financial institutions to make credit available at 7.00%.
(c) Prompt Repayment Incentive (PRI): An additional interest subvention of 3.00% per annum is provided to farmers who repay their short-term crop loans promptly on or before the due date.
(d) Effective Interest Rate: For prompt paying farmers, the net effective interest rate is only 4.00% per annum (7.00% minus 3.00% PRI).
(e) Overdue & Non-Repayment: If a loan is not repaid by the designated due date, the 3% PRI is forfeited and standard commercial/penal interest rates apply to the overdue balance.

5. SECURITY, COLLATERAL AND FEE NORMS
(a) Collateral-Free Limit: No collateral security or third-party guarantee is required for agricultural loans up to Rs. 1,60,000 (extended up to Rs. 3,00,000 under tie-up arrangements).
(b) For loans exceeding Rs. 1,60,000, hypothecation of standing crops and equitable mortgage of agricultural land (or charge created on land records via Bhulekh / RoR) serves as security.
(c) Fee Waiver: RBI and Indian Banks Association (IBA) have mandated zero processing fees, zero documentation charges, and zero ledger inspection charges for KCC applications up to Rs. 3.00 lakh.

6. APPLICATION PROCEDURE AND REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
Farmers can apply for KCC at any commercial bank, Regional Rural Bank (RRB), or Primary Agricultural Credit Society (PACS):
(a) Filled one-page simplified KCC Application Form.
(b) Proof of Identity: Aadhaar Card or Voter ID.
(c) Proof of Land Ownership / Cultivation: Copy of Record of Rights (RoR / 7/12 extract / Khasra-Khatauni).
(d) Sowing declaration or Land Cultivation Certificate verified by local Patwari / Talathi / Revenue Officer.
(e) Declaration of existing loans across all financial institutions to prevent duplicate financing.
(f) Timelines for Sanction: Banks are mandated to process and issue KCC cards within 14 working days of receiving a completed application.
